garden

The main idea of creating a garden is to use exclusively local ingredients in the creation of dishes and drinks, which will definitely be the hallmark of the restaurant’s menu.

The beautification of the designated area may help attract additional guests who may not have planned to visit a gourmet restaurant by selling this area for exclusive events:

  • local fashion shows, an exhibition of arts by artists
  • the presentation of music albums
  • wedding ceremonies

In addition to the very fact of holding such events, we will use staff to serve events using the catering system: with buffet tables, light snacks or even an off-site bar without involving third-party organizations, which will increase the profitability of the entire complex.
