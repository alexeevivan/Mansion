The territory, as well as the building on it, presented in your video, inspire silence, sophistication and some elite privacy. In this regard, I suggest that we consider this area not as a stand-alone restaurant, but rather to shift the focus to the very fact of the feeling of being here. Thus, I am putting forward the option Maison Privé as the name.

In this case, the combination of the words Maison and Privé work very organically, because it promises not just a dinner, but a stay in a special space. This, in my opinion, is the strongest side of this project.

The focus is on the fleur of a country residence — and that’s exactly what the word Maison conveys better. Maison allows to build a brand much further:

  • It will allow dividing the restaurant and the garden into separate structures:
  • the very fact of dividing into separate divisions opens up additional possibilities: maximum simplicity for memorization & the opportunity to attract more guests by holding events that are not associated with the classic concept of «restaurant».
  • for the restaurant, I suggest using the Privé Restaurant name.
